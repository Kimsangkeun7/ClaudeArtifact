# The "th" Sound (/θ/ and /ð/)

Two sounds spelled "th": voiceless /θ/ ("think") and voiced /ð/ ("this"). The tongue lightly touches the upper teeth.

Common substitution for Korean L1: /s/ for /θ/ ("think" → "sink") and /d/ for /ð/ ("this" → "dis").

Tricky in clusters like "months". A frequent target alongside the [[r-l-distinction]] when isolating broad pronunciation issues.
