# Articles (a / an / the)

English requires articles before most singular countable nouns. Korean has no articles, so learners frequently omit them ("I sent email" → "I sent an email").

Rules of thumb: `a/an` for first mention or non-specific, `the` for shared/specific reference ("the attached file").

Often surfaces alongside [[present-perfect]] accuracy issues under speaking pressure.
