# R / L Distinction

/r/ and /l/ are distinct phonemes in English but map to similar sounds in Korean, causing merges ("collect" vs "correct", "rice" vs "lice").

/l/: tongue tip touches the ridge behind the teeth. /r/: tongue pulls back, touching nothing.

Often the slower-to-fix of the two main pronunciation targets, the other being the [[th-sound]]. Drill with minimal pairs and slow-then-fast shadowing.
