# Listening Comprehension

The skill of following spoken English in real time. Stamina matters: many learners track short clips but lose detail past 3-4 sentences.

Common causes: unknown vocabulary mid-passage, fast connected speech, and not "chunking" meaning.

Build with graded clips, summary practice, and targeted vocabulary. Strong listening underpins exam sections and natural conversation.
