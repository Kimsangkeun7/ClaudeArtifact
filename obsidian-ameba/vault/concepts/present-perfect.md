# Present Perfect

Form: `have/has + past participle`. Used for past actions with present relevance, life experience, and unfinished time periods ("since 2019", "for three years").

Common Korean-L1 error: using simple past where present perfect is required ("I work here since 2019" → "I have worked here since 2019").

Related: [[articles]], and contrasts with the simple past in narrative. See also conditional backshift under [[conditionals]].
