# Business Email Tone

Direct grammatical correctness can still read as blunt ("Send the file now."). Softeners make requests polite without being wordy.

Useful patterns: "Could you...", "When you get a chance...", "I'd appreciate it if...", "Just following up on...".

Korean learners often need [[articles]] attention in emails too. Phrasal verbs (see [[phrasal-verbs]]) keep the tone efficient.
