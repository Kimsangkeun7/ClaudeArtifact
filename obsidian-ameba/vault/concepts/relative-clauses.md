# Relative Clauses

Clauses that modify a noun using who/which/that/whose. Defining clauses (no commas) vs non-defining (with commas, use "which" not "that").

Key rule: the relative pronoun can be dropped when it's the OBJECT, but not when it's the SUBJECT ("the man that called" — can't omit "that").

Frequently paired in exam prep with [[conditionals]].
